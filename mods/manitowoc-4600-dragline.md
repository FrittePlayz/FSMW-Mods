## What it is

This is not a download yet. The machine is in testing — pre-release builds go to the members' Discord first, and the picture above shows the model itself; in-game shots follow once it is out of testing.

What it does: the bucket hangs on two real ropes, hoist and drag line, each with its own physics. It tips from its geometry rather than an animation, and Dig With Anything recognises it as a digging tool.

Three boom lengths — 100, 120 and 140 ft. Slew and luff by mouse, hoist and drag on keys, and a crane automation that runs the cycle on a key press. Pendant ropes between gantry and boom tip are real ropes too.

Where it stands: the build loads clean in game, the first play test is done, and the details (feet, chains on the ground, tuning) are being worked through with testers.
