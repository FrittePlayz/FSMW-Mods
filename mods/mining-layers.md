## What it is

Mining Layers stays free. It was, it is, it will be.

Layers work without a drawn area. The reference height is frozen from undisturbed ground the first time a bucket touches a spot, so layers follow the terrain anywhere on the map. Set the target to “Everywhere” on the layers page. Where an area is assigned to the machine, that area still wins.

The target height is yours again. Level a pad at ground level, cut a berm halfway up the pit wall, grade a ramp at a fixed slope — the mod no longer pulls that height down by itself. Where your height cuts off the lowest layer, it says so and leaves the decision to you.

Slot diagnosis with your map's real numbers: how many ground slots the map has, how many are taken, how wide its channel is. Both numbers are read from the running game, and the mod says when they are full.

Requires TerraFarm, installed and active — that's the foundation, not a recommendation.

The free version was developed for FS25 and will not be continued beyond that.
