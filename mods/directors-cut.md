## What it is

The Director's Cut of Mining Layers ships as a drop series: transition band, material hardness with caprock, yield, an editor, and a mixed bucket.

Every drop as soon as it's done — no waiting for one big release. Member channels on Discord follow progress between drops.

This is its own build of Mining Layers — you install it instead of the free version. The two cannot run at the same time. Your layers and reference heights carry over, and so does the way back.

TerraFarm still has to be installed and active, same as for the free version.
