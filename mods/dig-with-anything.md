**About the download key:** the mods check a signed key file that your account issues. The check runs locally in the mod, offline; nothing is sent anywhere while you play. Part of the code in the zip is encrypted — that is the mod's own gameplay code, because our scripts turned up on other sites with the code altered. It is not a program: no .exe, no .dll, just Lua, XML and images.

## What it is

Grapples take from the barge, hold, dump into the truck — and lose material when you slew too fast.

Every tool shows up on the equipment list with its own icon, an ESC page with a store image, manufacturer and mod name, and a reload chip.

Automatic grapple detection reads a machine's i3dMappings — most clamshell grapples work without hand-tuning.

This is gameplay you see in the truck bed, not a script running in the background.
