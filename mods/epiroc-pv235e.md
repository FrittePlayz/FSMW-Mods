## What it is

This is not a download yet. It is a model in the making, and the pictures here are renders, not screenshots.

The rig is built to the dimensions of the published datasheet of its class — 12.6 m wide, 5.6 m deep, 26.5 m tall with the mast up. It stands next to a mining excavator at the size it would really have.

What it is meant to do once it runs: raise the mast, set a drill pattern, and drill each hole to a target depth. What comes out of the hole is Mining Layers' job — rock, deposit, or plain ground.

Where it stands: the model is far enough along to export as a valid i3d scene. Next is a placeable test mod in the game, then detail.
